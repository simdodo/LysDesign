Artist-led work often starts with references that are emotional rather than technical. The job is to understand what the artist is actually responding to: contrast, darkness, colour, tension, movement, nostalgia, aggression, stillness or a specific stage picture.

Once that is clear, the references need to become a system. Cues, angles, stage objects and fixture choices have to be rehearsed and repeated. The visual language should still feel like the artist when the room changes, the schedule compresses or the available rig is not the ideal one.
