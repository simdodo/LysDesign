Previs is most useful when it answers a production question. It can test whether a camera angle works, whether a stage object blocks a beam, whether the depth reads on screen, or whether a lighting idea needs a different fixture position before the team spends time in the room.

The level of detail should match the decision. Sometimes a rough stage environment is enough. Sometimes the production needs fixture positions, camera views, screen placement or reference frames. The point is not to replace the venue; it is to arrive with fewer expensive unknowns.
