Dance lighting works when the cue understands the body. A fade can be technically clean and still be wrong if it lands against the breath, weight shift, silence or focus of the performer. That is why rehearsal time is valuable: timing decisions are made with the work, not imposed after it.

For remounts and touring pieces, the design needs a memory. Focus notes, cue intention and practical fixture priorities matter because a new black box will not give the same trim, masking or sightlines. The goal is to keep the timing and attention intact even when the room changes.
